# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent to the people you are talking to. This is a small project about
joining video files together; nothing here is worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

- Assume the other person is trying to help, not to waste your time.
- Keep criticism about the code, not about the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or harassment of any kind.
- Comments about someone's background, nationality, religion, gender,
  orientation, disability, age or appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or issues.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions
and commit messages.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately through
[GitHub's report form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) if it concerns
a person rather than the code.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block accounts that
do not follow this document. Serious or repeated cases lead to a permanent ban.

This document is a short, plain-language adaptation of the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
